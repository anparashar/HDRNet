# HDRNet
A computationally efficient and lightweight hybrid deep residual network for fundus image denoising

Abstract: During the process of acquiring biomedical images, various types of noise are introduced into the images. Denoising process of the biomedical image has become a most captivating research area nowadays. Noisy Fundus creates problem during analysis process which makes diagnosis problematic. Therefore, it is required toreducenoises withoutdiminishingimage features (blood vessels, optic disk, macula, hemorrhage, and exudate). In this research, a novel network structure called Hybrid deep residual network (HDRNet) is proposed. Hybrid layer block consists combination of convolution, dilated convolution and separable convolution layer with batch normalization. HDRNet based on residual learning and it has the advantage of being lightweight and computationally efficient because of dilated and separable convolution. Its structure is more functional for the job of image denoising. Extensive testing shows that HDRNet performs superior than the state-of-art models in terms of MSE, PSNR and SSIM on two types of noise, one is additive in nature (Gaussian Noise) and other is multiplicative in nature (Speckle noise) and it is lightweight and computationally effective as well. There are numerous practical applications for this method in the realm of biomedical image processing.
Keywords: Fundus image; Denoising; Dilated deep convolution; Separable convolution; Gaussian and speckle noise
